# AEGIS NEW HIRE IT & CLOUD ONBOARDING GUIDE
Document ID: IT-ONB-2026-V1
Department: IT Operations & People Team

1. WELCOME TO AEGIS ENTERPRISE
Welcome aboard! On your first day, your primary IT objective is to configure your corporate workstation, authenticate your Okta identity, and establish encrypted access to your team resources.

2. DAY 1 SETUP CHECKLIST
2.1 Unbox hardware and power on. Connect to your local Wi-Fi.
2.2 Setup Apple ID / Microsoft Account using your corporate email (@aegis.enterprise).
2.3 The device will automatically enrol in Aegis Mobile Device Management (MDM - Jamf / Microsoft Intune).
2.4 Sign in to https://login.aegis.enterprise to establish your primary password and register your Okta Verify MFA on your mobile device.
2.5 Launch the Aegis App Catalog and install mandatory productivity apps: Slack, Zoom, 1Password, Google Chrome, and WireGuard VPN Client.

3. DEVELOPMENT ENVIRONMENT PROVISIONING
3.1 Engineering teams receive automated GitHub Enterprise invitations with single sign-on mapping.
3.2 AWS sandbox credentials are provisioned through AWS IAM Identity Center (AWS SSO) at https://aegis.awsapps.com/start.
3.3 Never commit AWS access keys or personal tokens to repositories. Use `aws sso login` to retrieve temporary session credentials.

4. GETTING HELP
If you encounter any blockers during onboarding, you can reach out to our AI Assistant 24/7 or join the #it-onboarding-help channel on Slack.
