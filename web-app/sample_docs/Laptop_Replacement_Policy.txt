# AEGIS HARDWARE REFRESH & LAPTOP REPLACEMENT POLICY
Document ID: HW-POL-2026-V3
Page: 12
Section: Hardware Replacement & Refresh Lifecycle
Department: IT Operations & Procurement

1. LAPTOP REPLACEMENT ELIGIBILITY & SCHEDULE
1.1 Standard Hardware Lifecycle:
All full-time employees are eligible to request a laptop replacement after 36 months (3 years) of continuous active service with their existing assigned device.
1.2 Early Replacement Conditions:
An employee may request an early replacement prior to 36 months under the following certified conditions:
a) Irreparable hardware failure certified by IT Support Desk diagnostics.
b) Physical battery expansion or severe battery degradation (maximum capacity below 60% with diagnostic evidence).
c) Documented job title change or engineering role requiring upgraded compute, RAM (minimum 64GB), or GPU capabilities.
d) Irretrievable accidental damage covered under enterprise device insurance.

2. STANDARD HARDWARE TIERS & MODELS
2.1 Tier 1 - Software Engineering & Data Science:
- Apple MacBook Pro 16-inch (M3 Pro/Max, 36GB Unified RAM, 1TB SSD)
- Dell XPS 16 / Precision 5680 (Intel Core i9, 64GB RAM, RTX 4070, 1TB SSD)
2.2 Tier 2 - Product, Design & Marketing:
- Apple MacBook Pro 14-inch (M3 Pro, 18GB RAM, 512GB SSD)
- Dell XPS 15 (Intel Core i7, 32GB RAM, 512GB SSD)
2.3 Tier 3 - General Corporate (Finance, Legal, HR, Operations):
- ThinkPad T14s Gen 5 / Surface Laptop 6 (16GB RAM, 512GB SSD)

3. HOW TO SUBMIT A REPLACEMENT REQUEST
3.1 Submit a request via the Aegis AI Support Portal:
- Navigate to "Create Ticket" and select category "Hardware & Devices".
- Choose priority "MEDIUM" (or "HIGH" if device is completely inoperable).
- Provide current device serial number and date of original issue.
- Department Manager approval is automatically routed through the AI workflow.
3.2 Upon approval, IT Procurement will dispatch the new device within 3 to 5 business days with pre-installed enterprise MDM profiles.
3.3 The previous laptop must be backed up to corporate OneDrive/Google Drive and returned using the prepaid shipping kit within 14 calendar days.
