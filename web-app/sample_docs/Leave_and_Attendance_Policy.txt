# AEGIS EMPLOYEE ANNUAL LEAVE & ATTENDANCE REGULATIONS
Document ID: HR-POL-2026-V2
Department: Human Resources & People Operations

1. PAID TIME OFF (PTO) ENTITLEMENTS
1.1 Standard Accrual: Full-time employees accrue 20 days (160 hours) of Paid Time Off per calendar year, accrued at 1.66 days per completed calendar month.
1.2 Seniority Multiplier: Employees with over 3 years of tenure accrue 25 days of PTO annually.
1.3 Rollover Cap: A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year, expiring on March 31st.

2. SICK LEAVE & CAREGIVER LEAVE
2.1 Employees receive 10 days of fully paid sick and mental wellness leave each year.
2.2 Consecutive sick absences exceeding 3 business days require a medical clearance certificate submitted to HR confidential records.

3. PARENTAL & BEREAVEMENT LEAVE
3.1 Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave.
3.2 Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of 100% paid parental leave.
3.3 Bereavement leave provides up to 5 consecutive paid days for immediate family members.

4. REQUESTING TIME OFF
4.1 Requests should be submitted in the Workday portal at least 2 weeks in advance for planned leaves exceeding 3 days.
4.2 Urgent absences must be reported to the team manager and logged in the Aegis Employee Portal prior to 09:00 AM local time.
