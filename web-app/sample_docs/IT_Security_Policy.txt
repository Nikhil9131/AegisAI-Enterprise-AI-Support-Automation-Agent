# AEGIS ENTERPRISE IT SECURITY POLICY (2026 REVISION)
Document ID: SEC-POL-2026-V4
Classification: INTERNAL CONFIDENTIAL
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Approved By: Information Security Office & Chief Information Officer

1. PURPOSE & SCOPE
This document outlines the mandatory information security controls for all Aegis Enterprise employees, contractors, and authorized third parties. These policies apply to all corporate hardware, cloud infrastructure, and network assets.

2. AUTHENTICATION & MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)
2.1 All employees must authenticate using Okta Single Sign-On (SSO) backed by FIDO2 WebAuthn or push verification.
2.2 SMS-based verification is strictly deprecated and prohibited for corporate authentication.
2.3 Passwords must meet minimum complexity standards: at least 14 characters, alphanumeric with special symbols, and must not match the last 5 previous passwords.
2.4 Passwords expire every 90 days for administrative and privileged accounts.

3. WORKSTATION HARDWARE SECURITY & COMPLIANCE
3.1 All corporate laptops and workstations must run approved endpoint detection and response (EDR) software (CrowdStrike Falcon).
3.2 Full disk encryption (FileVault on macOS, BitLocker on Windows 11) must remain enabled at all times.
3.3 Workstation screens must automatically lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
3.4 Non-compliant devices are automatically isolated into the Quarantine VLAN and blocked from internal services until remediated by IT Ops.

4. REMOTE WORK & ZERO TRUST ACCESS
4.1 Direct SSH and RDP access over the public internet is strictly forbidden.
4.2 All remote network traffic to staging and production clusters must traverse the Aegis WireGuard Global VPN with Mutual TLS (mTLS).
4.3 Split-tunneling is restricted: corporate internal subnets (10.240.0.0/16) and cloud VPCs are routed through the VPN tunnel.

5. INCIDENT REPORTING & SECURITY BREACH NOTIFICATION
5.1 Any suspected phishing attempts, stolen hardware, or anomalous login alerts must be reported within 60 minutes to security@aegis.enterprise or via the Aegis AI Assistant.
5.2 Unsolicited credential prompts or macro-enabled documents should be immediately forwarded to security-ops@aegis.enterprise for sandbox detonation.
